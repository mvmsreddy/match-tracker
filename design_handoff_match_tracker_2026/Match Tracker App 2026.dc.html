The rules every screen follows

Surfaces

Page ground is tinted, cards are the light/dark surface at 24 px radius with a soft shadow (day) or 1 px border (night). Hero blocks invert: ink card on paper, gradient card on black. No hairline grids.

Type

Manrope only. Headings 800 at −0.03em, body 500–600 at 14–15 px, sentence case. IBM Plex Mono is reserved for live scores and set boxes — never for labels.

Controls

Pill segments replace tabs. Primary actions are 54 px tall, 20 px radius. Wizard chips are 92 px tiles with a secondary line. Nav is a floating bar on phone, a labelled rail from 1024 px.

Contrast law

Every colour has a fill value and a separate ink value, and they are never swapped. Lime, amber and blue are fills — as text they darken to #4E6B10, #8A5F00, #1B5FBF. Nothing on a lime fill is ever white; nothing on a dark surface is ever near-black. Every pair in these frames clears 4.5:1 (3:1 for large display text) — audited, not eyeballed.

Colour meaning

Lime = you / primary. Terracotta = opponent & destructive. Amber = forced error & practice ring. Blue = hydration & info. Every status also carries a letter or glyph, so sun-washed screens still read.
